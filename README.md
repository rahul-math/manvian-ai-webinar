# Manvian: AI-Powered Manufacturing Webinar Landing Page

> **"AI-Powered Manufacturing: Building the Smart Factory of Tomorrow"**  
> An executive webinar landing page built for **Manvian** to communicate how Artificial Intelligence is transforming traditional manufacturing into intelligent, automated, and data-driven industries.

---

## 🎨 Color Palette & Aesthetic

- **Active Palette: Cyber Amber & Electric Cyan (Industrial Robotics & Smart Automation)**
  - **Primary Background**: Deep Carbon Space (`#0A0D16`)
  - **Secondary Background**: Midnight Steel (`#101524`)
  - **Primary Accent**: Electric Industrial Amber / Gold (`#F59E0B`)
  - **Secondary Accent**: Electric Cyan (`#38BDF8`)

  2. 🔵 **Cobalt Blue** (Aerospace & Enterprise Systems)
  3. 🟣 **Neural Violet** (Deep-Tech AI & Autonomous Intelligence)
  4. 🟢 **Matrix Emerald** (Cleanroom & Zero-Defect Industry 4.0)
  5. 🔴 **Crimson Titanium** (High-Velocity Heavy Manufacturing)

---

## 🌟 Key Features & 10 Core Sections

1. **Sticky Navigation Bar (`Navbar.jsx`)**: Brand emblem with pulsing node, interactive Theme Palette Switcher, smooth scroll navigation anchors, and responsive mobile menu.
2. **Hero Section (`Hero.jsx`)**: Split layout with live countdown timer to 28 November 2026, dual CTAs, trust indicator, and real-time robotic vision viewfinder with laser scanline beam and animated telemetry waveform.
3. **About the Webinar (`About.jsx`)**: "The Future of Manufacturing Is Intelligent" interactive pillar explorer with live telemetry readouts (FFT vibration spectrum, RUL estimation, defect cycle times).
4. **What You'll Learn (`LearningOutcomes.jsx`)**: 4 technical modules (Predictive Maintenance, Computer Vision QA, Smart Factory Automation, Production Optimization).
5. **Webinar Agenda (`Agenda.jsx`)**: 90-minute structured timetable (11:00 AM – 12:30 PM IST) with active session inspector and slide deck delivery badge.
6. **Speaker Profile (`Speaker.jsx`)**: Arjun Mehta (Industrial AI & Automation Consultant), 12+ years field experience, track record statistics ($18M+ prevented downtime), and domain tags.
7. **Why Attend? (`Benefits.jsx`)**: 5 core benefits with cards and attendee bonuses (PDF Blueprint, Certificate of Attendance, 1080p Replay).
8. **Registration Section (`Registration.jsx`)**: High-converting enterprise form with real-time green check verification indicators, input validation, confetti celebration, and instant **Google Calendar** & **Download iCal (.ics)** ticket generation.
9. **Interactive FAQ (`FAQ.jsx`)**: Accordion covering fees, audience, prerequisites, recordings, and access instructions.
10. **Footer (`Footer.jsx`)**: Manvian brand identity, quick links, contact hotline & email, security certificates (SOC-2 Type II), and legal notice.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework**: React 18
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS 3 (Dynamic CSS Custom Property Variable Engine)
- **Icons**: Lucide React
- **Animations & Effects**: Laser scanlines, audio/vibration waveforms, canvas confetti, glassmorphism
- **Build Tool**: Vite 6
